# Monad Metropolis submission draft

**Project:** ViralKit  
**Tagline:** One referral identity. Every Monad app. Instant rewards.  
**Primary track:** Social, Attention & Culture (Track 3), per the build brief. Confirm the portal's current track names and eligibility before submitting.  
**Project description:** ViralKit gives Monad apps a shared, onchain referral identity and a small viem SDK for campaign checkout. Merchants create independent campaigns. A confirmed purchase atomically pays the merchant and referrer, and the same referral identity can be used across separate apps. The testnet demo includes newsletter access and an event pass, a merchant console, and an Envio-powered activity view.
